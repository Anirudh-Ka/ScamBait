# ScamBait: 2-minute demo script

Setup before judges arrive: app open on the start screen, sound on, Gemini + ElevenLabs + Nessie keys live. Pick **Rosa**, language **Spanish** (or English if the judge doesn't read Spanish), difficulty **Convincing**. Have a second tab already in a round as a backup.

## 0:00 to 0:15, the hook

> "Americans lost a record 15.9 billion dollars to fraud last year, and almost half of it went to fake investments. Everyone knows the tips. They don't work, because scams don't feel like scams in the moment. So we built an app that scams you on purpose."

## 0:15 to 0:35, personalization

Point at the four people.

> "Scammers pick targets, so you pick who you are. Rosa is 71, on a fixed income, and reads best in Spanish. Gemini writes a whole day of messages aimed at her specifically, in her language."

Click **Start the day**.

## 0:35 to 1:05, the core loop

Open the **bank text**. Tap **Looks safe** on purpose.

> "Say Rosa believes it."

The money-sent screen appears.

> "That money really left. Every player has a Capital One Nessie sandbox account, and that was a real transfer to a scammer account. Here's what she missed."

Tap **Show me the red flags**. Point at the highlights and the field notes.

> "Every red flag is highlighted with the exact words and why it matters. Gemini wrote this scam, but we verify every quote against the message before we show it."

## 1:05 to 1:35, the call

Go back to the inbox. The phone rings within a couple of seconds.

> "And then the phone rings."

Answer. Let the ElevenLabs voice play for 5 seconds. Point at the captions. (Gemini writes a new call every round, so adapt the line to whoever is calling.)

> "That's her grandson asking for bail in gift cards. AI voice cloning makes this scam far more convincing now. Live captions are always on."

Tap **Talk back**, tap the suggested reply "What's our family code word?". The scammer deflects.

> "The scammer is Gemini, playing it the way real scammers do. Watch the pressure meter."

Tap **Hang up, report**, pick **Keep it quiet** and **Odd payment**, submit.

## 1:35 to 2:00, debrief and mission

Finish the remaining messages quickly (report or trust), then open **See how you did**.

> "At the end you see which tactics work on you, highlighted in yellow, plus three habits to keep and where to report fraud for real."

Open **Display**, flip **Largest text** and **High contrast**.

> "We designed for the people scammers target most: older adults, non-native speakers and people with low vision. That's a low-vision typeface, text up to 150 percent, high contrast, read-aloud, captions, and ten languages."

## Likely judge questions

- **"Couldn't this teach people to scam?"** Gemini only writes the target's side, everything is fictional, links can't be clicked, and real senders never roleplay. It's the same content as a bank's phishing training, made personal.
- **"How do you know Gemini is accurate?"** Structured output with a schema, quotes verified against the message, explanations constrained to general rules (the SEC never approves investments, no court takes gift cards). If anything fails validation it's dropped, and hand-written packs back it up.
- **"Where's the T. Rowe angle?"** All three areas in one product: fake ETFs teach fees and "guaranteed" returns, fake tokenized funds teach custody and wallet risk, and AI shows up both as the threat (bots, voice clones) and as the teacher (personalized training).
- **"What's the business?"** Banks and brokerages already pay for phishing training for employees. This is that for customers, offered at account opening or bundled with fraud alerts.
- **"How is Nessie used beyond reading data?"** Each player gets a newly created customer and account, purchases are written to it, and every scam payment is a P2P transfer. In "play as my own customer" mode, Gemini uses your real purchase history in the scams.
